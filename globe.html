    How Voyage plans
    What the map shows, and what it estimates.

    Voyage plans a trip around the places you list, then gathers real things to do at each one.

    Drive trips use real road routing from open mapping data (OpenStreetMap), pace overnight stays by the hours-per-day you choose, and surface sights along the way. Distances and times are estimates, not live traffic.

    Fly and Boat trips draw a direct line between the places you list. That is a straight-line estimate for planning, not a booked airline route or a shipping lane, and it carries no time estimate.

    Stays, things to do, events, and videos at each stop come from connected platforms (hotels, Ticketmaster, YouTube) when their keys are set, and from open searches otherwise. Nothing is faked.

    This is general trip-planning information. Book and confirm details with the real services before you travel.
